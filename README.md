📖 ABOUT THE GAME

Welcome to Pumpkin Panic! You are a little pumpkin trapped inside a haunted forest on Halloween night. Survive terrifying creatures, collect magical pumpkins, avoid dangerous traps, and fight to stay alive until sunrise.

Can you survive the night?

✨ GAME FEATURES

* 🎃 Collect magical pumpkins and earn points.
* 👻 Escape ghosts and terrifying enemies.
* 💀 Survive against skeletons and monsters.
* ❤️ Health and life system.
* ⚡ Special powers and power-ups.
* 🌙 Increasing difficulty levels.
* 🏆 Scoreboard and high-score tracking.
* 🔊 Spooky background music and sound effects.

🛠️ TECHNOLOGIES USED
